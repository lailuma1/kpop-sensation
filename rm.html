❤️ About RM

RM, whose real name is Kim Namjoon, is the leader and one of the rappers of BTS. He is known for his songwriting, lyricism, leadership, public speaking, artistic interests, and love of literature and art.

As BTS's leader, RM has played an important role in communicating with fans and representing the group internationally.

Outside of BTS, he has developed a distinctive solo identity focused on introspection, art, creativity, and personal expression.

🎵 RM's Solo Career

🎧 RM — 2015

RM released his first solo mixtape, RM, in 2015. The project gave listeners a deeper look into his personal thoughts, identity, and artistic style outside BTS.

🎧 mono. — 2018

His playlist mono. explored themes of loneliness, reflection, comfort, and emotional growth. It became an important project in RM's solo discography.

🎨 Indigo — 2022

Indigo was RM's first official solo studio album. The album connected music with art, memory, identity, and different stages of his life.

🌿 Right Place, Wrong Person — 2024

RM released his second solo studio album, Right Place, Wrong Person. The project explored individuality, identity, freedom, and the complicated feeling of being between different worlds.

🎼 RM's Artistic Style

RM's music often combines hip-hop with alternative, indie, pop, and experimental influences. His lyrics frequently explore identity, self-reflection, uncertainty, relationships, art, society, and personal growth.

His solo work also frequently connects music with visual art and cultural ideas, creating projects that feel more like personal journals than simple collections of songs.
