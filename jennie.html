The Human Chanel ✨

Jennie Kim is a South Korean singer, rapper, performer and fashion icon. Known for her powerful charisma, confidence and luxury style, Jennie has inspired millions of fans around the world.

👑 About Jennie

Jennie debuted with BLACKPINK in 2016. She became the first BLACKPINK member to debut as a solo artist. Her performances, fashion, and confidence have earned her worldwide recognition. Fans often call her the "Human Chanel" because of her elegant luxury style.

🏆 Career Highlights

  • 🖤 Member of BLACKPINK.
  • 🎤 First BLACKPINK member to release a solo song.
  • 🌍 Global ambassador for luxury fashion brands.
  • 🏆 Multiple music awards with BLACKPINK.
  • 💎 One of the most influential Korean idols worldwide.
