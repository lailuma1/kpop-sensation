💜 About BTS

BTS is a South Korean boy group formed by BIGHIT MUSIC. The group debuted in 2013 and consists of seven members: RM, Jin, SUGA, j-hope, Jimin, V, and Jung Kook.

The group became known around the world for their music, performances, storytelling, and connection with their fans, known as ARMY.

Their music has explored themes such as youth, self-discovery, dreams, friendship, love, identity, and personal growth.
