💜 About BTS

BTS is a South Korean boy group formed by BIGHIT MUSIC. The group debuted in 2013 and consists of seven members: RM, Jin, SUGA, j-hope, Jimin, V, and Jung Kook. Over the years, BTS has achieved worldwide recognition through their music, performances, messages, and connection with their fans, known as ARMY.
