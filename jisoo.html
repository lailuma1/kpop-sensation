👑 Kim Jisoo 👑

Miss Korea of K-pop 💜

Kim Jisoo is a South Korean singer, actress, performer, and member of BLACKPINK. She is admired for her elegant visuals, charming personality, and graceful stage presence. Fans around the world often call her "Miss Korea of K-pop", a popular fan nickname that reflects her beauty and elegance.

🌸 About Jisoo

Born on January 3, 1995, Kim Jisoo debuted with BLACKPINK in 2016 under YG Entertainment. Besides music, she has built a successful acting and fashion career and is known for her confidence, kindness, professionalism, and timeless style.

🏆 Achievements

  • 💜 Member of BLACKPINK, one of the world's biggest girl groups.
  • 🌸 Released her successful solo debut album ME.
  • 🎵 "FLOWER" became an international hit.
  • 🎬 Starred as the lead actress in Snowdrop.
  • 👑 Global ambassador for luxury fashion and beauty brands.
  • 🏆 Winner of multiple music awards with BLACKPINK and as a solo artist.
  • 🌍 One of the most influential Korean celebrities worldwide.
